# Resume Tips for Freshers & Professionals - Karya Saarthi

## Key Tips:
1. ATS Friendly: Keep layout clean and avoid complex multi-column tables.
2. Action Verbs: Start bullet points with words like Led, Built, Designed, Implemented.
3. Quantify Results: Mention numbers (e.g., "Increased performance by 20%").
4. Customization: Tailor your resume to the job description.

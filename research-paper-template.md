# Research Paper Template - Karya Saarthi

Use this standard format for structuring academic research papers.

## 1. Title & Abstract
- Clear, concise research title.
- Structured abstract (Background, Objectives, Methodology, Results, Conclusion).
- 4 to 6 relevant keywords.

## 2. Introduction
- Background information and context.
- Problem statement and research gap.
- Significance and scope of the study.

## 3. Literature Review
- Critical summary of prior research.
- Theoretical framework.

## 4. Methodology
- Research design and sampling technique.
- Data collection instruments and tools.
- Data analysis methods.

## 5. Results & Discussion
- Key findings with charts and tables.
- Comparison with existing studies.

## 6. Conclusion & References
- Summary of implications and future scope.
- References in APA / IEEE / MLA format.

# Thesis Writing Guide - Karya Saarthi

A step-by-step guide to complete your thesis on time.

## 1. Topic Selection & Proposal
- Choose a specific and feasible problem statement.
- Define research questions and objectives clearly.

## 2. Literature Review
- Use Google Scholar and ResearchGate.
- Keep track of citations using tools like Zotero or Mendeley.

## 3. Methodology & Analysis
- Detail your research design and data collection methods.
- Document qualitative or quantitative analysis steps.

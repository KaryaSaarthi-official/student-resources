## What is Plagiarism?
Using someone else's words, ideas, or data without appropriate citation or attribution.

## Best Practices to Avoid Plagiarism:
1. *Paraphrase Effectively:* Rewrite ideas in your own words while retaining original meaning.
2. *Cite Accurately:* Always provide standard in-text citations and bibliographic references.
3. *Use Quotations:* Put exact phrases inside quotation marks with page numbers.
4. *Self-Check:* Use verified plagiarism detectors prior to submission.
